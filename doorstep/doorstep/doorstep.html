Frequently asked questions.

Do I pay when I submit the order form?

No. Submitting the form places your order — it doesn't charge you anything. Once we confirm your details, we'll send secure payment instructions by email or text message, and printing begins after that payment is received.

How long does it take for the door hangers to be delivered?

Once payment is confirmed, printing takes about a week and hand-delivery across your radius takes another two to three — plan on 3–4 weeks total from payment to your last hanger dropped.

Is the design of the door hanger included?

Yes. Send us your logo and any branding you have, and our design team will send back up to 3 layout variations sized exactly to the slot you purchased — no extra fee.

Is the tracking service free?

Yes. Each hanger carries a unique QR code, and you get dashboard access showing scans by day, by zip code, and by device — included at no extra cost.

What if I want fewer than 2,000 door hangers?

2,000 is our minimum print run — it's what keeps the per-hanger cost low. Above that minimum, the configurator lets you scale up in steps of 1,000.

Can I control which streets or neighborhoods get a hanger?

Your radius sets the outer boundary, and our delivery team routes within it for the best coverage. If specific streets need to be excluded, your account rep can adjust the route after signup.
