Why door hangers work

Built for a visible, comparison-shopped trade

Fences are outdoors, in plain view, and easy to compare block by block.

Advertise past the fence that needs you

Every leaning post and gray, weathered panel on a street is a homeowner who hasn't called anyone yet. A hanger on that exact door starts the conversation.

A cheaper way to reach a niche search

Fence installation searches are lower-volume but high-cost per click in many markets. A hanger reaches the same homeowner for a fraction of the cost, without waiting on ad rank.

Shows the fence, not just a quote

A photo of a finished cedar or vinyl fence on the hanger lets a homeowner picture their own yard before you ever walk the property line.

Timed to spring projects and storm damage

Run a batch in early spring when yard projects get planned, or right after a wind or storm event that knocks down panels across a neighborhood.

After a windstorm took down fences on three streets, we papered the area with hangers. Half our replacement jobs that month came straight from that batch.
