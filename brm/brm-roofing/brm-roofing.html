    Questions

    Frequently asked questions.

    Do I pay when I submit the order form?

    No. Submitting the form places your order — it doesn't charge you anything. Once we confirm your details, we'll send secure payment instructions by email or text message, and printing begins after that payment is received.

    How long does it take for door hangers to be delivered?

    Once payment is confirmed, printing takes about a week and hand-delivery across your radius takes another two to three — plan on 3–4 weeks total from payment to your last hanger dropped.

    Is the design of the door hanger included?

    Yes. Every order includes our basic ad design package at no charge — send us your logo and branding and our team builds a layout sized exactly to the slot you purchased. Want something fully custom and unique to your brand instead? Add the premium design package for $90.

    Is the tracking service free?

    Basic QR code tracking is included with every order — we'll update you when you request a look at your campaign's performance and progress. Want scan-by-scan detail on your own schedule? Add the QR code web dashboard for $79 to see when, how many times, and where your ad gets scanned, any time you like.

    What if I want fewer than 2,000 door hangers?

    2,000 is our minimum print run — it's what keeps the per-hanger cost low. Above that minimum, the configurator lets you scale up in steps of 1,000.

    Can I control which streets get a hanger?

    Your radius sets the outer boundary, and our delivery team routes within it for the best coverage. Street-by-street exclusions can be handled by your account rep after signup.

    Do you deliver outside my city, or nationwide?

    BlockRadius works with small businesses across the country. Wherever your business address is, we build your radius from that point.
