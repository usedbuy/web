Frequently asked questions.

Is this actually free?

Yes. You get 100 professionally printed door hangers at no cost. The only charges are for the options you choose — hand delivery, shipping, premium design, QR tracking, or a website package — and those are optional.

Do I pay when I submit the form?

No. Submitting the form places your order — it doesn't charge you anything. If you selected any paid options, we'll send secure payment instructions by email or text message before printing begins.

What are my delivery options?

We can hand-deliver your 100 hangers directly to nearby doors for $22, ship them to your business address for $14.99, or you can pick them up yourself at no charge.

How does local pickup work?

Pickup is available every Saturday between 10 AM and 3 PM at our location in Vancouver, Washington. We'll email you the exact pickup address once your order is ready.

What if I don't want to design the ad myself?

Add the premium design package for $90 and send our team your logo, colors, and any info you want featured — we'll design a fully unique, company-branded ad for you before printing.

How does the QR code web dashboard work?

For $79, we add a unique QR code to your hangers and give you access to a web dashboard showing when, how many times, and who scans it. You choose the forwarding URL — the page customers land on after they scan.

Can I order more than 100 door hangers?

Not right now — quantities above 100 are currently sold out in your city. This offer covers your first 100 door hangers; reach out and we'll let you know if larger runs become available.
