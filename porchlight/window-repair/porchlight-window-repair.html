Details & logistics

Everything else you need to know.

Do I pay when I place my order?+

No. Placing an order here doesn't charge anything — it just gets your campaign started. Once you approve your design proof, we'll send a payment request, and your door hangers go to print after that's settled.

How long does it take for the door hangers to be delivered?+

Once payment is confirmed after you approve your design, it usually takes 3–4 weeks for all door hangers to be printed and hand-delivered across your chosen radius.

Is the design of the door hanger included?+

Yes. Our design team builds up to 3 free variations for your ad slot, so you can pick the one that fits your brand before anything prints.

Is the tracking service free?+

Yes. Every campaign includes a free dashboard where you can watch scans on the QR code printed on your door hangers, in real time.

Can I change my radius or ad size after signing up?+

You can adjust either one any time before your design is finalized. Once printing begins, changes apply to your next campaign instead.

Do you offer runs larger than 20,000 hangers?+

Pricing scales the same way at any size — there's no markup as you grow. For runs beyond 20,000, contact us and we'll put together a custom quote.
